# Product Scope — CBE Interschool Examination Management

## In scope

- Multi-council / examination-organiser administration.
- School registry, approval and participation.
- Teacher identity, school memberships and assignments.
- Learner identity and assessment-specific candidate records.
- Grades 1–9 curriculum and configurable learning areas/rubrics.
- Internal and external assessment configuration.
- Candidate registration and import.
- Marks entry, validation, correction history and submission.
- Review, approval, result release and audit.
- School-scoped and organiser-scoped analytics, reports and exports.
- Email/SMS notifications and payment capabilities where required by approved business flows.

## Explicit exclusions

- Admissions.
- Learner-created accounts or learner login in the current scope.
- Strand/substrand/outcome/evidence-level mark capture.
- Automatic publication immediately after marks entry.
- Payment success based only on frontend state.

## Product experience principles

1. Optimize for educator task completion, not dashboard decoration.
2. Make high-volume mark entry keyboard-efficient and recoverable.
3. Show what is incomplete, what needs attention and what happens next.
4. Preserve context during search, filtering, editing and correction.
5. Explain validation errors beside affected data.
6. Design bulk-entry workflows for desktop while keeping review and administration responsive on tablets and phones.
7. Apply WCAG 2.2 AA principles.
8. Provide clear empty, loading, error, success and permission-denied states.
